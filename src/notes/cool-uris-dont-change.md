> What makes a cool URI? A cool URI is one which does not change. What sorts of URI change? URIs don't change: people change them.

Tim Berners-Lee, [Hypertext Style: Cool URIs don't change](https://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI), 1998. Leave the file extension out, leave the CGI directory out, leave the author's name out, leave anything out that a future reorganization might invalidate. A good [[values|value]] to have.

This site's slugs follow this rule. `notes.11tydata.js` derives every URL from the file's slug alone, so a note keeps its address for as long as the filename does, independent of category, theme, or any future retagging.
