A collection of favorite sans-serif typefaces spanning humanist, grotesque, and geometric classifications for user interfaces, branding, and display applications.

## Humanist Sans-Serif

### [Lato](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lato)
Humanist sans-serif by Warsaw-based designer Łukasz Dziedzic (2010). "Lato" means "Summer" in Polish. Semi-rounded details for warmth with strong structure. Nine weights with italics.

### [Radio Canada](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Radio+Canada)
Humanist typeface by Charles Daoud with Coppers and Brasses (2017) for CBC/Radio-Canada. Brand-unifying information font with distinctive angles and subtle curves. Won three awards in 2018.

### [Radio Canada Big](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Radio+Canada+Big)
Variable font with weight axis (400-700), part of Radio Canada family. Designed for larger display applications while maintaining humanistic style and legibility.

## Grotesque & Neo-Grotesque

### [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)
Neo-grotesque sans-serif by Rasmus Andersson at Figma (2017). Crafted for computer screens with tall x-height and open apertures. Variable font with weight and optical size axes. Widely used in applications and websites.

### [Source Sans 3](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3)
Sans-serif by Paul D. Hunt for Adobe (2012), their first open-source font family. Inspired by News Gothic and Franklin Gothic with larger x-height. 2020 update added Greek and Cyrillic to italics.

### [Public Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Public+Sans)
Fork of Libre Franklin by U.S. Web Design System, designed by Dan Williams (2019). Free open-source webfont for government websites with tailed lowercase 'l' and regularized stroke weight.

### [Miranda Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Miranda+Sans)
Contemporary sans-serif by Max Thunberg, added to Google Fonts in 2026. Open forms and low stroke contrast for clear reading. Variable font with weight axis (400-700).

## Geometric & Rounded

### [Nunito](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito)
Sans-serif superfamily with rounded terminals by Vernon Adams (c. 2010). Extended by Jacques Le Bailly with Nunito Sans added by Julieta Ulanovsky. Friendly, approachable warmth popular in education and healthcare.

## Brand & Display

### [Instrument Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)
Variable sans-serif by Rodrigo Fuenzalida for Instrument brand. Balances precision with playfulness, inspired by neo-grotesques. 12 stylistic sets and variable axes for width, weight, and italic slant.

### [Mozilla Headline](https://github.com/mozilla/mozilla-headline-type)
Custom typeface for Mozilla by Studio DRAMA. Uniwidth design maintaining standardized spacing across all weights and styles, eliminating text reflow. Part of Mozilla type family.

### [Stack Sans Headline](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Stack+Sans+Headline)
Variable sans-serif by Koto for Stack Overflow's rebrand. Tight spacing and strong optical correction for headline sizes. Weight axis spanning six instances (ExtraLight to Bold).
