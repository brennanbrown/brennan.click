Publish (on your) Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere: Post to a domain you control first, then push copies to whatever silos your readers already use, each copy pointing back at the canonical original. 

The [IndieWeb wiki](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) frames it against PESOS, publishing elsewhere and copying back, where a platform outage means you simply can't post.

The pitch is ownership. If Twitter, Medium, or any platform in between disappears tomorrow, [[values|the notes]] here don't go with it. A site that outlasts feed readers and aggregators.
