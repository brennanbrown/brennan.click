[Andy Matuschak argues that evergreen note titles work like an API](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_note_titles_are_like_APIs): a well-factored, well-titled note lets you reference its entire argument through the title alone, the same way a function signature lets you use code without re-reading its body. Separation of concerns, sharp declarative phrasing, and positive framing are the "API design" techniques for getting there.

That maps directly onto [[atomic-notes|atomicity]], if a note's claim can't be compressed into a title without losing something essential, that's usually a sign it's covering more than one idea and should split.

Filenames on this site lean closer to nouns than claims, `atomic-notes` rather than something like "notes should be atomic." 
This is a concession to how the [[eleventy-interlinker]] resolves slugs into URLs.
