[A map of content is a note that's mostly links](https://www.dsebastien.net/2022-05-15-maps-of-content/), a curated index into a cluster of [[atomic-notes|atomic notes]] on one topic, sitting alongside folders and tags rather than replacing them.

Automatic groupings can't make judgment calls the way a hand-written map can. A folder or a tag can only say a note belongs somewhere; a map of content can say why, and can put the same note in more than one place at once.
